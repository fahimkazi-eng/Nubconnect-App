## Real campus events (from nub.ac.bd)

Twelve event listings under "Official — from nub.ac.bd" (Events page, Home
teaser) preserve NUB's published feed: titles verbatim, each linking to its
original article (`https://nub.ac.bd/nub/event/…`). Feed:
https://nub.ac.bd/nub/events. The feed lists dates as "Mon DD"; the year 2026
is inferred from same-listing titles and documented in
`src/lib/nubEvents.ts`. Venue/start times are not published there, so they
are not shown. Upcoming demo events remain clearly labelled DEMO.

## Policy

- Every demo person, room, product, conversation, and notification in this
  app is labelled **DEMO**. Real students are never fabricated.
- No stock photography is used for rooms or products.
- No AI-generated people, no fabricated university photos, events, or
  identities. Authentic NUB photography always carries its source.
- The generated SVG art intentionally does **not** depict real, identifiable
  individuals. Person avatars are stylized silhouettes.
- Students own their own profile pictures — nothing in the NUB asset
  registry is used for people's avatars.
- When a university backend (Supabase) is connected, real content replaces
  the demo dataset and real uploaded photographs are served from object
  storage.
